Small websites for serious local businesses.

pHouse builds straightforward, well-made websites for service businesses whose reputation deserves a better first impression online.

The offer is deliberately modest: a fast static site, clear writing, practical maintenance, and no overbuilt agency theatre. We research the business first, use public assets carefully, and build the site by hand.

What we do, and what we avoid.

Most small-business websites do not need a platform, a funnel, or a quarterly strategy retainer. They need to look trustworthy, load quickly, explain the service, and make contact easy.

Research before layout

We look at the business name, reviews, service area, existing photos, colours, logo, and nearby competitors before making design choices.

Plain writing

No inflated slogans. The site should say what the business does, where it works, why customers trust it, and how to get in touch.

Quiet maintenance

Clients email small updates. pHouse handles them. No fragile CMS login unless there is a real reason for one.
