Terms

Plain-English terms for the pHouse website service.

Service

pHouse Productions provides small-business website design, deployment, hosting, and maintenance services.

Scope and pricing

  • Pricing and scope are confirmed before work begins.
  • Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime.
  • Annual payments are paid upfront and are not retroactively refunded.
  • One reasonable content update per month is included unless otherwise agreed.
  • Client-provided content, logos, photos, and domain access remain the client’s responsibility.

Domains

Clients may use their own domain or ask pHouse to manage a domain for the agreed annual fee. Domains can be transferred after the account is settled and registrar rules allow.

Contact

For questions, email hello@phouseweb.ca.
